# Annapurna Marketing Strategy & Content Calendar

---

## Positioning
**"Ghar jaisa healthy atta, diabetics ke liye banaya"**

Not a supplement. Not a medicine. It's **atta your family already eats** — just smarter.

---

## 4 Content Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Education** | Why low GI matters, what's in the atta | 3x/week |
| **Social Proof** | Customer reviews, delivery photos, testimonials | 2x/week |
| **Behind the Scenes** | Packing process, family story, quality check | 1x/week |
| **Recipes** | Roti, dosa, paratha using diabetic atta | 2x/week |

---

## Channel Strategy

| Platform | Role | Content Type |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp Business** | Sales closer | Catalog updates, status, broadcast |
| **Instagram** | Awareness + trust | Reels, stories, carousels |
| **Facebook** | Local reach + groups | Posts, group sharing, events |

## Growth Tactics (Beyond Ads)

| # | Tactic | How | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Doctor referrals** | Visit 3-5 local diabetologists in Lalbaug/Parel, leave samples + pamphlets | ₹500 samples |
| 2 | **WhatsApp Broadcast** | Weekly broadcast to all inquiries (new recipe, offer, tip) | Free |
| 3 | **Instagram Reels** | 30-sec recipe videos, "What I eat in a day" diabetic edition | Free |
| 4 | **Facebook Groups** | Join "Mumbai Diabetics", "Healthy Food Mumbai" groups, share value posts | Free |
| 5 | **Referral program** | "Refer a friend, both get ₹25 off next order" | ₹50/referral |
| 6 | **Google My Business** | Register Annapurna for local search | Free |
| 7 | **Local WhatsApp groups** | Housing societies, mom groups in Kalachowki/Lalbaug | Free |
| 8 | **Pamphlet drop** | 500 pamphlets in local societies + medical stores | ₹1,500 |

## Instagram Reels Ideas (Viral Potential)

2. "₹250 vs ₹30 doctor visit" (cost comparison)

4. "Packing 1kg atta at 6 AM" (behind the scenes, ASMR-style)
5. "What diabetics should eat vs what they actually eat" (myth busting)

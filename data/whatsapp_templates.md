## Funnel Strategy

1. **Hook** — Lead with price + free delivery, NOT ingredients
2. **Social proof** — "Already delivered to happy customers"
3. **Engagement** — Ask for pincode (gets a reply, not a ghost)
4. **Bundle push** — 2kg for ₹500 (saves ₹100, increases AOV)
5. **Close** — "Send address, I'll confirm order"

## Promo Code
- **FIRST25** — ₹25 off first order (tracks ad-driven conversions)
